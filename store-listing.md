## Short description (80 chars max)

## Full description
**The opposite of addiction is connection.**

HoldOff is your calm companion for the 3–60 seconds before you send a message you might regret. Write your draft, and Sadie — a warm, judgment-free AI — gives you a clear verdict:

- **CLEAR** — send it, you're good.
- **HOLD** — take a breath; Sadie offers a gentler rewrite that keeps your meaning.
- **SPIRAL LOCK** — only for real escalation signals, with immediate support options.

**Also included:**
- **Interpreter** — paste a message you received and understand its tone, what it might mean, and healthy ways to reply.
- **Reflect** — look back at a conversation and spot patterns, calmly and without labels.
- **Sadie Chat** — a companion to talk things through, anytime.

HoldOff never shames, never diagnoses, and never pretends to be therapy. Your drafts stay on your device; only the text you choose to analyze is sent for review.

Take back the pause. Mean what you say.

## Data safety (for Play Console form)
- Data collected: message text the user explicitly submits for analysis (optional, user-initiated)
- Data shared: submitted text is sent to the AI service provider solely to generate the analysis; no sale of data
- Data stored on device: drafts, chat history (AsyncStorage, user-deletable via in-app "delete everything")
- No account required. No contacts accessed. No SMS accessed.
