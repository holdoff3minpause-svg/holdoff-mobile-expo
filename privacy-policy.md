# Privacy Policy — HoldOff

**Last updated: October 14, 2026**

HoldOff ("we", "our", "the app") is a messaging companion app by Stacy Ann Martin, DBA HoldOff.

## What we collect

**On your device only (never leaves your phone unless you ask):**
- Message drafts you write
- Sadie chat history
- App preferences and onboarding state

You can delete all of this at any time from inside the app ("delete everything").

**Sent for analysis only when you tap it:**
When you explicitly ask Sadie to review a draft, interpret a message, reflect on a conversation, or chat, the text you submit is sent to our AI service provider solely to generate that response. We do not sell this data. We do not use it for advertising.

**We do NOT collect:**
- Your contacts, SMS messages, or call history
- Your location
- An account, name, or email (the app requires no sign-in)

## How we use information

Solely to provide the app's features: draft verdicts, message interpretation, conversation reflection, and companion chat.

## Data retention

Submitted texts are processed to generate your response and are not retained by us beyond that. On-device data persists until you delete it.

## Children

HoldOff is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We will update this policy here if our practices change.
